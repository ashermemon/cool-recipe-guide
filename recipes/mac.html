Mac and Cheese

Creamy, cheesy stovetop mac and cheese made from scratch.

Ingredients

  • Elbow macaroni
  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Cheddar cheese, shredded
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Boil macaroni according to package instructions, then drain.
  2. Melt butter in a pot over medium heat.
  3. Whisk in flour and cook for 1 minute to form a roux.
  4. Slowly whisk in milk until smooth, then simmer until thickened.
  5. Stir in shredded cheese until melted and smooth.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Add macaroni to the cheese sauce and stir to coat.
  8. Serve warm and enjoy!
