Apple Pie

A classic homemade apple pie with a warm, spiced apple filling.

Ingredients

  • Pie crust
  • Apples, peeled and sliced
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon
  • Nutmeg
  • Lemon juice
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Line a pie dish with the bottom pie crust.
  3. Toss sliced apples with sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice.
  4. Pour apple mixture into the crust and dot with small pieces of butter.
  5. Cover with the top crust and cut a few slits to vent steam.
  6. Bake for 45-50 minutes until the crust is golden brown.
  7. Let cool before slicing and enjoy!
