Amazing Lasagna Recipe

A classic, cheesy baked lasagna layered with meat sauce and noodles.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Marinara sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. Brown ground beef and mix with marinara sauce, salt, and pepper.
  4. Mix ricotta cheese with the egg.
  5. Layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella in a baking dish, repeating until ingredients are used up.
  6. Top with parmesan cheese and cover with foil.
  7. Bake for 25 minutes, then uncover and bake 10 more minutes until golden.
  8. Let cool for 10 minutes, then serve and enjoy!
