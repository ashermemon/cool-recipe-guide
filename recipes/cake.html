Rich Chocolate Cake

A moist, rich chocolate cake with a smooth chocolate frosting.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Cocoa powder
  • Baking soda
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chocolate frosting

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a cake pan.
  2. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa powder, and baking soda in a bowl.
  3. Add eggs, milk, and vegetable oil, then mix until smooth.
  4. Pour batter into the cake pan.
  5. Bake for 30-35 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Let the cake cool completely.
  7. Spread chocolate frosting over the top.
  8. Slice and enjoy!
