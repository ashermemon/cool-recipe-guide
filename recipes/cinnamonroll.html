Sweet Cinnamon Rolls

Soft, gooey cinnamon rolls topped with a sweet cream cheese glaze.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough or crescent dough
  • Butter, softened
  • Brown sugar
  • Cinnamon
  • Cream cheese
  • Powdered sugar
  • Splash of milk

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Roll out dough into a flat rectangle.
  3. Spread softened butter over the dough.
  4. Mix brown sugar and cinnamon, then sprinkle evenly over the butter.
  5. Roll the dough up tightly and slice into rolls.
  6. Place rolls in a baking dish and bake for 15-20 minutes until golden.
  7. Mix cream cheese, powdered sugar, and milk to make the glaze.
  8. Drizzle glaze over warm rolls and enjoy!
