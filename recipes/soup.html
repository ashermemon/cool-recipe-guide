Chicken Noodle Soup

A cozy homemade chicken noodle soup with tender vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken breasts or thighs
  • Chicken broth
  • Egg noodles
  • Carrots, sliced
  • Celery, sliced
  • Onion, diced
  • Garlic, minced
  • Olive oil
  • Thyme, salt, and pepper
  • Fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add onion, carrots, and celery and cook for 5-7 minutes until softened.
  3. Stir in garlic and thyme and cook for 1 more minute.
  4. Pour in the broth, add the chicken, and bring to a boil. Lower the heat and simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Take out the chicken, shred it with two forks, and return it to the pot.
  6. Add the egg noodles and cook for 6-8 minutes until tender.
  7. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  8. Top with fresh parsley and serve hot!
