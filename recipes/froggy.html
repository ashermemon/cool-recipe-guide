Sweet Flexbox Froggies

Roasted Organic flex-box froggies

Ingredients

  • Lily-pad froggies
  • Justify-content
  • Align-items
  • A pinch of flex-wrap (so no froggy falls off the pan)
  • Flex-direction, row (not column they hop sideways)
  • Butter, softened
  • Order: 1

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and set display: flex on the baking tray.
  2. Arrange froggies in a flex container, setting flex-direction: row so they line up neck to neck, not stacked.
  3. Apply justify-content: space-evenly so every froggy gets equal roasting time, no favorites.
  4. Set align-items: center to keep every froggy sitting pretty on the pan, none sagging off the edge.
  5. Brush with softened butter and sprinkle on the flex-wrap so stragglers don't hop off the tray.
  6. Bake for 15-20 minutes, checking that no froggy has set itself to order: -1 and snuck to the back of the line.
  7. Let cool, then plate with a nice gap between each one for presentation.
  8. Serve immediately, these froggies wait for no one
